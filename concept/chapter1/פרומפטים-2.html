פרומפט
The camera is completely static, looking down from the ridge over the town, with the dark cloth-draped stone cube at the centre of the plaza below.

Everything gradually stills. The moving people fade softly away until the plaza is empty. Then, one by one, the standing idol stones at the edges of the plaza dissolve quietly into nothing — not exploding, not falling, simply thinning away like dust taken by wind, each a few seconds after the last.

When they are all gone, the plain stone cube remains, alone in the empty plaza, unchanged and untouched.

The light shifts imperceptibly warmer as this happens. No other movement.

Photorealistic, cinematic, restrained, contemplative. Slow and quiet, not dramatic.

Audio: the town's murmur fades to nothing. Only wind remains. No music, no voice.

ExtendCIN 6 — המצלמה עולה אל המפה
Extend CIN58s
המעבר למפה עצמה נעשה אצלי בעריכה. מ־Flow אני צריך רק את ההתרוממות, שתיגמר גבוה מספיק כדי שאוכל להמיס אותה לתוך המפה.
פרומפט
The camera begins to rise slowly and steadily straight upward from the ridge, pulling back and up away from the town, the plaza and the dark cube growing smaller below.

It keeps rising until the whole valley is visible, then the ranges beyond it, then the desert stretching away in every direction to a hazy horizon. The rise is smooth, continuous and unhurried, and does not stop.

The image becomes progressively flatter, hazier and more abstract as height increases — like a landscape becoming a drawing.

Photorealistic becoming atmospheric, cinematic, high detail, contemplative.

Audio: wind thinning into near silence as the camera rises. No music, no voice.

פרומפט
Match the valley, the town and the composition of the reference image exactly — the same place from the same high vantage.

Change the time: it is now late sunset, almost dusk. The sun has just gone behind the mountains, the sky is deep orange fading to violet at the top, and the valley is in cool shadow. Lamps and small fires are beginning to appear one by one among the mud-brick houses below. The dark cloth-draped cube at the centre of the plaza is the darkest thing in the valley.

The camera holds almost still, drifting very slightly and slowly downward toward the town.

Photorealistic, cinematic, high detail, quiet and final.

Audio: evening wind, very distant town sounds settling, one dog barking far away. No music, no voice.
